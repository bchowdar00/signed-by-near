## 4. M7 completion (METHOD M7; DEVIATIONS 22)
Use the `contiguous` part of `nd/blocks.jsonl` (2,000 blocks).
- **Requests** are `sign` calls whose block is in the middle 1,800 blocks (blocks 101 to 1,900 of the run, by height order). **Responses** are `respond` calls that succeeded.
- **Pairing key:** (canonical payload as lowercase hex, tweak, domain).
  - For a request, the tweak = SHA3-256 of `"near-mpc-recovery v0.1.0 epsilon derivation:" + predecessor + "," + path`. Use `hashlib.sha3_256`.
  - For a response, take the tweak from its args.
  - A request of the older argument form (byte-array payload with `key_version`) is domain 0.
- **Pairing order.** Identical requests pair in order with responses at or after them, each response used once. Label those pairs "by order".
- **Ambiguous:** a request whose repeated-request group has a member in the first 100 blocks.
- **Censored:** a request with no response by the end of the run.

Report:
- blocks;
- evaluated, matched, matched unique, matched by order, ambiguous and censored;
- failed responses seen, and unmatched responses;
- the matched share = matched ÷ evaluated, and matched ÷ (evaluated − ambiguous);
- the median and longest delay in seconds, over matched pairs.

First verify your tweak derivation on a few requests whose tweak is unique in the run, and report how many matched by tweak alone.
